# Available tools in 0.1.4

This page lists the tools available in the 0.1.4 release. Each one runs in the browser or through Local Studio.

## Viewer plugins

Available from **Plugins** in the viewer.

**4D Schedule.** Turn IFC tasks and schedule CSVs into a live construction sequence.

**Clash Detection.** Turn raw clashes into an auditable coordination docket.

**Element Explorer.** A spreadsheet over every element and property in the model.

**IDS Requirements Studio.** Write and standardize information requirements.

**Model Compare.** See what moved, reshaped, changed level or changed data.

**Model Finder.** Search the whole model in plain words, then act on what comes back.

**Model Health.** Catch model identity and geometry problems before review.

**Point Cloud.** As-built scan over the model, coloured by deviation.

**Presentation.** Saved views as an ordered walkthrough, played or recorded.

**Python Console.** Write IfcOpenShell against the open model.

**Quantity Takeoff.** Volumes, areas and counts rolled up by class and storey.

**Report Builder.** Free-form schedules: your columns, your grouping, saved as a template.

**Room Book.** Every space with its area, volume and occupancy.

**Rule Studio.** The checks IDS cannot express: geometry, hosting, placement and quantities.

**Section Workspace.** Turn the live 3D cut into a readable drawing.

**Sheets.** The issued drawings, calibrated and linked to the model.

**Smart Measure.** Put object gaps and room axes on the model.

**Storey Navigator.** Walk the building one level at a time.

## Built-in tools

Available directly from the viewer.

**Element Schedules.** Tabular exports with resolved property columns.

**IDS Validation.** Check the model against a buildingSMART specification.

**Issue Tracker.** Keep BCF local or sync it with an OpenCDE project.

**Model Checks.** Structural QA over the whole file.

**Model Edits.** Rename, set properties and delete, with a measured diff.

**Smart Filters.** Rule based visibility that you can stack and undo.

## Local Studio

Available after installing Local Studio.

**Assistant Key Vault.** Keep the provider key off the browser.

**IfcOpenShell Converter.** Exact solids and instant reopens.

**MCP Bridge.** Let Claude and other AI clients drive this viewer.

**Native Python.** Full IfcOpenShell scripting with no runtime download.
